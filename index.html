About Me

My name is Zavier Dollaway and I am a senior in the Multidisciplinary Engineering Technology program at Texas A&M, focused in Mechatronics. I am interested in dexterous robotics and engineering education which has led me to co-found the Dexterous Design Challenge (DDC) for the Human AugmentatioN via Dexterity Engineering Research Center (HAND ERC). On top of my coursework, I also serve as the Founding President of the ASME student chapter for the Engineering Technology and Industrial Distribution Department. I am looking for new and exciting challenges to continue to grow my knowledge of robotic systems and allow me to inspire others to pursue engineering as well.
