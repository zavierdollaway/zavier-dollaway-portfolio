Dexterous Design Challenge

Educational Leadership

In a joint effort with my organization Vice-President, we partnered with the Human AugmentatioN via Daxterity Engineering Research center to create the Dexterous Design Challenge. Multidisciplinary engineering teams of 4-6 people were given 8 weeks to design, build, and test their 11-DOF robotic hands. Supported through learning modules, students of all skill levels and backgrounds were able to compete. With the success of the previous competition, the HAND ERC has supported us in taking the competition to a national scale.
